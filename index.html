Our Legacy

A Century of Empowering Minds

Delhi University Library System (DULS) is a federally managed combination of physical and virtual collections and associated information services. Established in 1922, the University Library has grown into one of the largest academic library systems of the country.

The library commenced its journey with 1,380 gift books and 86 periodicals. The Central Library Building was inaugurated on 1 December 1958 by President Dr. S. Radhakrishnan. Dr. S.R. Ranganathan, father of modern library science in India, was among the moving spirits behind its development. Today, DULS encompasses 34 libraries across both campuses, actively supporting teaching, learning, and research.
